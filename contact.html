Contact Us

We're a small team and we genuinely read every message we receive. Whether you've found a bug, have a suggestion for a new calculator, or just want to share feedback — we'd like to hear from you. Your input directly shapes what we build next.

Reasons to Get in Touch

🐛 Bug Reports Found a calculation that doesn't look right, a broken button, or a display issue? Please let us know. Include the calculator name and what you expected to see.
🧮 Calculator Suggestions Is there a business calculation you need that we don't currently offer? We're actively expanding the tool set and prioritise suggestions from real users.
✏️ Content Feedback If an explanation is unclear, a formula description is confusing, or an FAQ doesn't answer your actual question — tell us. We want every page to be genuinely useful.
📊 Formula Accuracy If you believe a formula or calculation method is incorrect or could be improved, we want to know. We take accuracy seriously and will investigate every report.
📚 Guide Suggestions Have a business finance topic you'd like us to cover in a guide? We're building out the Guides section and welcome topic suggestions.
💬 General Feedback Anything else — a feature request, a usability observation, or just a note to say a tool was helpful. All feedback is welcome and appreciated.

How to Reach Us

Before You Write

To help us respond as quickly and usefully as possible, please include the following in your message where relevant:

  • For bug reports: The name of the calculator, the inputs you entered, the result you got, and what you expected to see instead
  • For formula accuracy concerns: The specific formula or result you believe is incorrect, and a reference or source for the correct version
  • For calculator suggestions: A brief description of what the calculator would do and why it would be useful
  • For content feedback: The page URL and the specific section or sentence you found unclear or incorrect
⏱ Response Time We aim to respond to all enquiries within 48 hours on business days (Monday–Friday). During busy periods, it may take a little longer. We do not offer phone support — email is the only contact channel.
