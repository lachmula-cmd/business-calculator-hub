Getting Started

How to Use These Calculators

Every calculator on Business Calculator Hub is designed to be used in under two minutes. Here's how to get the most out of them:

  1. Choose the right calculator for your question. The category headings above group them by use case.
  2. Enter your real numbers. The quality of the result depends entirely on the accuracy of your inputs. Use actual figures from your accounts where possible.
  3. Read the explanation sections below each calculator. They explain what the result means, how to interpret it, and what to do next.
  4. Use the related calculators linked at the bottom of each page. Many metrics are most useful when compared — for example, CAC is most meaningful when compared to LTV.
  5. Share or copy your results using the buttons in the result box. Useful for sharing with a co-founder, accountant, or investor.
Why It Matters

Why Accurate Business Calculations Matter

Many small businesses fail not because of a bad product or poor service, but because of poor financial visibility. When you don't know your true profit margin, you can't price correctly. When you don't know your break-even point, you can't set a realistic sales target. When you don't know your CAC and LTV, you can't make rational decisions about marketing spend.

The calculators on this site are built around standard, widely accepted financial formulas used by accountants, investors, and business analysts. They won't replace a qualified accountant for complex decisions, but they give you the financial literacy to ask the right questions, spot problems early, and make better day-to-day decisions.

Every number you understand about your business is a decision you can make with confidence rather than guesswork.
