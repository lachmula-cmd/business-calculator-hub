How to use

  • ARPU: Average amount a customer pays you per month.
  • Gross Margin: The percentage of that revenue that is profit.
  • Churn Rate: The percentage of customers cancelling each month.

FAQ

What is LTV?

Lifetime Value (LTV) predicts the net profit attributed to the entire future relationship with a customer.

Formula

LTV = (ARPU * Gross Margin %) / Churn Rate %
