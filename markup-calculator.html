FAQ

How is Markup Calculated?

The formula is: ((Selling Price - Cost) / Cost) * 100.

Difference between Markup and Gross Margin?

Markup is calculated based on cost. Gross Margin is calculated based on revenue (selling price). A 50% markup results in a 33% gross profit margin.
