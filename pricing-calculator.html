How to use

  • Unit Cost: Direct cost to make one product.
  • Overhead: Total fixed monthly expenses.
  • Expected Units: Conservative estimate of monthly sales volume.
  • Desired Margin: The percentage of the final price that should be pure profit.

Theory

This calculator ensures you don't underprice by forgetting overheads. It calculates the "Total Cost per Unit" first, then applies the margin formula.

Price = TotalCostPerUnit / (1 - Margin%)
