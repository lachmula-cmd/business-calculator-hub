Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 30, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Business Calculator Hub collects (very little), how it is used, and what your rights are. We've written this in plain language so it's actually readable.

1. Who We Are

Business Calculator Hub ("we", "us", "our") operates the website at businesscalchub.com. We provide free, browser-based business and financial calculators for informational purposes.

If you have any questions about this policy, you can contact us at: contact.uksalary@gmail.com

2. What Data We Collect

Calculator Inputs — Not Collected

All calculations on Business Calculator Hub are performed entirely within your web browser using JavaScript. The numbers you enter into any calculator are never transmitted to our servers. We do not store, log, or have any access to the figures you input. Your financial data stays on your device.

Usage Data — Collected Anonymously

Like most websites, we may collect basic, anonymised usage data through third-party analytics services. This may include:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • General geographic region (country or city level, not precise location)
  • Browser type and operating system
  • Referring website (how you found us)
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)

This data is aggregated and anonymised. It cannot be used to identify you personally. We use it solely to understand which calculators are most useful and how to improve the site.

Contact Enquiries

If you contact us by email, we will receive your email address and the content of your message. We use this only to respond to your enquiry. We do not add you to any mailing list or share your contact details with third parties.

3. Cookies

Business Calculator Hub may use cookies in the following ways:

Essential Cookies

These are necessary for the website to function correctly. They do not track you across other websites and do not store personal information.

Analytics Cookies

We may use analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) that set cookies to help us understand how visitors use the site. These cookies collect anonymised, aggregated data. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Advertising Cookies

This website uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of third-party vendor use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

4. How We Use Information

The limited information we collect is used for the following purposes:

  • To understand how the site is used and which tools are most valuable
  • To identify and fix technical issues or errors
  • To respond to contact enquiries
  • To improve the content, accuracy, and usability of the calculators
  • To display relevant advertisements through Google AdSense

We do not sell, rent, or trade any user data to third parties. We do not use your data for direct marketing.

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdSense — for displaying advertisements. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics (if enabled) — for anonymised usage analytics. Google Privacy Policy

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services. We encourage you to review their privacy policies directly.

6. Data Retention

Since we do not collect personal data through the calculators, there is no calculator-related data to retain or delete. Contact enquiries sent by email are retained only as long as necessary to resolve the enquiry and are not stored in any database.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights under applicable data protection law, including:

  • The right to access any personal data we hold about you
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate data
  • The right to request deletion of your data
  • The right to object to or restrict certain processing
  • The right to data portability

Given that we collect very little personal data, most of these rights will be satisfied by the fact that we simply don't hold data about you. If you have a specific request, please contact us at contact.uksalary@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Business Calculator Hub is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

9. Links to Other Websites

Our website may contain links to external websites (for example, links to Google's opt-out tools or official documentation). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external site you visit.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of the site after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
